# marketing:translate-content

Translate marketing content with automatic service routing per language pair, quality scoring across five dimensions (length ratio, formatting, key terms, placeholders, completeness), and a brand-voice check on the result; flags idioms, wordplay, and emotional CTAs for transcreation with 2-3 scored creative options plus back-translations. Triggers on \"/digital-marketing-pro:translate-content\", \"translate this landing page into German\", \"localize this email for India\", \"transcreate our slogan\", \"does the translated version keep our brand voice\". Reads the brand profile's do-not-translate terms, glossary, and formality preferences; executes via connected translation MCP servers.

**Skill folder:** `skills/digital-marketing-pro-translate-content/`
**Namespace:** `marketing`
