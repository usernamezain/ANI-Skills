# marketing:seo-plan

Build a 12-month SEO strategy and phased roadmap with industry templates (SaaS, ecommerce, local, publisher, agency). With fresh specialist outputs it runs as a dispatcher: scores four pillars — technical, content, topical, AI search — and makes the weakest pillar the plan's lead theme; missing specialists re-run only after explicit confirmation. Plans and prioritises only — makes no live changes. Triggers on \"/digital-marketing-pro:seo-plan\", \"build an SEO strategy\", \"what should our SEO roadmap be\", \"plan SEO for a new site\", \"which SEO pillar is weakest\". Reads specialist PLAN.md outputs (tech-seo-audit, content-decay-scan, aeo-audit, backlink-gap, keyword-cluster, seo-drift); feeds /digital-marketing-pro:content-engine and /digital-marketing-pro:campaign-plan.
