# marketing:image-seo-audit

Audit every image on a page for SEO, performance, and accessibility — alt text quality, tiered file-size thresholds, WebP/AVIF format adoption, srcset/sizes responsiveness, lazy loading, fetchpriority on the LCP image, and width/height for CLS — producing an optimization list sorted by file-size savings. Triggers on \"/digital-marketing-pro:image-seo-audit\", \"audit our image SEO\", \"check alt text across the page\", \"why are our images so heavy\", \"should we switch to WebP\". Fetches and analyzes the page via tech-seo-auditor.py and reads the brand profile for industry context.

**Namespace:** `marketing`
