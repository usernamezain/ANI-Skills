# marketing:focus-group

Run a simulated focus group: AI personas grounded in your CRM data react to messaging, pricing, or creative stimuli, producing a transcript with per-segment sentiment, consensus themes, objections, and explicit confidence caveats — directional hypotheses, not real consumer research. Triggers on \"/digital-marketing-pro:focus-group\", \"test this messaging before we spend on research\", \"how would our segments react to this pricing\", \"run a synthetic panel\", \"which positioning lands better\". Builds and reuses panels via audience-simulator.py, reads the brand profile, and shares panel IDs with /digital-marketing-pro:message-test.

**Skill folder:** `skills/digital-marketing-pro-focus-group/`
**Namespace:** `marketing`
