# marketing:share-of-voice

Calculate share of voice against named competitors across four dimensions — organic (volume-weighted keyword visibility), paid (Google Ads auction insights), social (mention volume with sentiment weighting), and AI citations across the six canonical AI surfaces — aggregated into a weighted SOV dashboard with gap-to-leader metrics and trend deltas. Triggers on \"/digital-marketing-pro:share-of-voice\", \"what's our share of voice\", \"how visible are we vs competitors\", \"are we winning the AI citation race\", \"who leads the category conversation\". Reads the brand profile and competitor baselines, persists each measurement via competitor-tracker.py for momentum tracking; social listening needs a connector via /digital-marketing-pro:add-integration.

**Namespace:** `marketing`
