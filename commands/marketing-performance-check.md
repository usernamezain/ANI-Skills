# marketing:performance-check

Pull live metrics from every connected analytics MCP into one cross-channel snapshot: KPI scoreboard with RAG status vs profile targets, period-over-period trends, industry benchmarks, top wins and concerns, and 3-5 recommended actions — then persist the snapshot via performance-monitor.py for trend history. Triggers on \"/digital-marketing-pro:performance-check\", \"how are our marketing metrics\", \"pull current KPIs\", \"quick performance snapshot\", \"are we hitting our targets\". Reads the brand profile for KPI targets and industry benchmarks; reports data gaps for unconnected platforms. Pairs with /digital-marketing-pro:performance-report, which turns these snapshots into the stakeholder narrative.

**Skill folder:** `skills/digital-marketing-pro-performance-check/`
**Namespace:** `marketing`
